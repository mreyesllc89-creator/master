FACTS THAT SHAPE THE TASK (BRIEF sections 1 to 5)
1. The live feed is 5 terminal global variables: XPW_XAU_OK, _HEARTBEAT, _FORCE_IMB, _NEAR_IMB, _BOOK_IMB. Each |imbalance| >= 5 is a vote worth +1.0 score; two agreeing votes set the flow direction. Without a feed the score tops out at 4.5 (a winner needs 4.0; the market route needs 5.0 and EntryRouterMode >= 1): only speed + body + micro-break can win, the winning side is always the burst side, and the market route (74% of my real trades) never fires. With a feed, flow creates entries, can flip the side and opens the market route. Live flow numbers change entries only; four exit branches read just the :FD label and never fired in 2,584 real trades. The tester having no feed is believed, not tested: item 1 of the MT5 checklist is a 10-line tester EA that prints GlobalVariableCheck("XPW_XAU_OK").
2. Entry is first passage over the ticks of an alert window. (Agent measurement, Oct 2026, shipped preset) 92.7% of decision ticks have no winner and 74% of those are one correct vote away from winning. So the feed must exist at tick resolution, piecewise constant between updates.
3. The feed cannot be rebuilt from prices: best out-of-sample AUC 0.54 to 0.59 on 1,260 real market entries, and the label does not predict the result (agree minus disagree -9.6 points, 95% range -26 to +7). So build a seeded generator of the three numbers and let the EA's own rules produce labels and routes. It is a stand-in, not a copy.
4. The real numbers exist in my terminal. The first log has force/near/book, flow_fresh, flow_dir and both scores at every decision tick, skips included (time to the second); the second, if my live build writes it, has the heartbeat age (update cadence) in milliseconds. 5. FlashGold is a breakout re-arming gate with no flow code (BRIEF section 2), so calibrate it first, on the neutral replay (flow off).
