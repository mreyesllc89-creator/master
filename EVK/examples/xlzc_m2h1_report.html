How to read this

  • In-sample (IS): data used to choose. Out-of-sample (OOS): data after the split, never used to choose. Only OOS numbers estimate future results.
  • Profit factor (PF): gross profit / gross loss. Above 1 makes money. A pass with no losing trade is capped at 100.
  • Base rate: share of all passes profitable out of sample. A selection method is useful only if its picks beat the base rate.
  • Neighbourhood: the pass plus the passes one optimization step away on each numeric input. Choosing by the neighbourhood median avoids isolated lucky peaks.
  • Walk-forward: choose on a training window, trade the next test window, roll forward, and join all test windows. It is the closest backtest to running the EA with periodic re-optimization.
  • Results come from the MT5 Strategy Tester runs in the input file. Check the tester model ("Every tick based on real ticks" is best) before trusting them.
