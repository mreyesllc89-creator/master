# FlashGold Direction Filter: accuracy search

Data: TradingView export CRYPTO:BTCUSD 5m. In-sample 2026-05-30 to 2026-08-22, out-of-sample 2026-08-22 to 2026-10-17 (never used to choose). Accuracy = buys and sells averaged, 50% = coin flip.

Searched: 5 filter timeframes x 8 readings x 11 conditions x 5 outcomes = 2200 candidates; 1550 had >= 150 in-sample calls. With this many tries, the best in-sample figures are inflated by luck: only the out-of-sample column counts.

**0 of the top 15 hold**: out-of-sample interval, re-sampled by whole signals, entirely above 50%, from at least 20 different signals. Calls made under the same signal overlap and move together, so the number of signals, not of calls, is the real sample size.

Outcomes: *close +h* = the close h filter candles later is on the called side; *1 ATR first* = price reaches 1 ATR on the called side before 1 ATR on the other (within 24 candles; both in one candle counts as wrong).
Conditions: *dist* = distance from the level in ATR of the signal candle; *signal age* = filter candles since the last signal; *vol* = ATR(14) over its 100-candle average.
