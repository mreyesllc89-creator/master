# FlashGold Direction Filter: accuracy search

Data: TradingView export OANDA:XAUUSD 5m (tick 0.001, 2H/4H candles from 21:00 UTC). In-sample 2026-03-27 to 2026-07-27, out-of-sample 2026-07-27 to 2026-10-16 (never used to choose). Accuracy = buys and sells averaged, 50% = coin flip.

Searched: 5 filter timeframes x 8 readings x 11 conditions x 5 outcomes = 2200 candidates; 1490 had >= 150 in-sample calls. With this many tries, the best in-sample figures are inflated by luck: only the out-of-sample column counts.

**0 of the top 15 hold**: out-of-sample interval, re-sampled by whole signals, entirely above 50%, from at least 20 different signals. Calls made under the same signal overlap and move together, so the number of signals, not of calls, is the real sample size.

Outcomes: *close +h* = the close h filter candles later is on the called side; *1 ATR first* = price reaches 1 ATR on the called side before 1 ATR on the other (within 24 candles; both in one candle counts as wrong).
Conditions: *dist* = distance from the level in ATR of the signal candle; *signal age* = filter candles since the last signal; *vol* = ATR(14) over its 100-candle average.

The full table of every candidate is in the `_all.csv` next to this report.

## Replication of the BTC lead (chosen on BTC, tested on all gold data)

"2H, signal reversed when the last signal is 9+ filter candles old" was the
only BTC candidate that stayed high out of sample (68-77%, 17-18 signals).
On gold, which played no part in choosing it:

| Outcome | Gold 2H | 95% by signal (36 signals) |
|---|---|---|
| close +1 | 49.3% | 40.0 - 58.8% |
| close +3 | 46.1% | 34.1 - 58.0% |
| close +6 | 49.2% | 34.4 - 62.3% |
| close +12 | 56.9% | 40.7 - 72.6% |
| 1 ATR first | 40.7% | 26.8 - 55.5% |

**Not replicated.** On BTC itself the same rule on 1H scores 38-46% (the
opposite side), so the 2H result looks like noise rather than an effect.
Parity: with the 2H / 4H candles starting at 21:00 UTC, the Python gate
matches TradingView's exported permissions on 99.98% of bars.
