Loma Out-of-Sample Review

The six gold presets were tested on prices they were never fitted to: VT Markets real ticks in MetaTrader 5 (Codex's run, 1 Mar – 6 Nov 2026), a second data source (Dukascopy), and a rolling walk-forward recalibration on FTMO data.

Verdict: do not trade these presets with real money.

The EA reproduces its backtest closely, so the code is not the problem. The edge the backtest showed did not continue after the data used to choose the presets: from June on, five of the six lose money even before commission and the sixth (Top02) is about break-even, on both VT and Dukascopy prices. Recalibrating every few months gives mixed results: one schedule out of five passes, but only on four windows that include the rally.

4 · Walk-forward recalibration

Recalibrating on recent months does not find a lasting edge

On FTMO data (Jan 2025 – mid-May 2026) I kept the method's fixed values (MACD 12/26, Stochastic 7 at 20/80, the hump rules) and let seven settings vary: 576 setups. Each window chooses the best setups on the past months, trades the next months untouched, then rolls forward. "Top 5" trades the five best setups together. "Stressed" adds a one-minute delay, slippage, commission and worst-case trailing.

The pass mark was PF above 1.2 on at least 100 untouched trades. Two of the 20 variants pass, and both are the same schedule: 9 months calibrate, 2 months trade, top 5 setups (PF 1.32, or 1.21 stressed, on 259 trades). That pass rests on only four windows, and two of them, Dec 2025 – Jan 2026 (+11.2R) and Feb – Mar 2026 (+28.1R), cover the gold rally; the other two made −6.9R and +1.3R. The other four schedules range from −19.7R to +21.4R, and about half of all windows lose.

5 · What this means

The EA is right; the pattern is not enough on its own

  • The EA is correct. On VT real ticks (Mar – mid-May), 100 of its 107 entries match a Python backtest entry within one bar, on the same side. Two independent price feeds also agree on the six-preset totals for the later months.
  • The backtest numbers came from fitting, mainly to the gold rally of late 2025 and early 2026. After that period the results lose even before costs (VT PF 0.74 before commission, Jun – Nov 6) and lose heavily after them.
  • Recalibrating is not a reliable fix. Only one of five recalibration schedules passes, on four windows that lean on the rally. A schedule that works only for one window length is more likely luck than an edge.
