XAUUSD · Loma TrendPullback v1.32/v1.33 · six presets with stressed backtest PF ≥ 1.3 in both 2025 and 2026

Loma Out-of-Sample Review

The six gold presets were tested on prices they were never fitted to: VT Markets real ticks in MetaTrader 5 (Codex's run, 1 Mar – 6 Nov 2026), a second data source (Dukascopy), and a rolling walk-forward recalibration on FTMO data. A full re-calibration of every timeframe on gold and BTC, with every kind of trailing stop and part of the data held back, followed.

Verdict: do not trade these presets with real money.

The EA reproduces its backtest closely, so the code is not the problem. The edge the backtest showed did not continue after the data used to choose the presets: from June on, five of the six lose money on VT even before commission and the sixth (Top02) is about break-even there; on Dukascopy prices all six lose. Recalibrating every few months gives mixed results: one schedule out of five passes, but only on four windows that include the rally.

4 · Walk-forward recalibration

Recalibrating on recent months does not find a lasting edge

On FTMO data (Jan 2025 – mid-May 2026) I kept the method's fixed values (MACD 12/26, Stochastic 7 at 20/80, the hump rules) and let seven settings vary: 576 setups. Each window chooses the best setups on the past months, trades the next months untouched, then rolls forward. "Top 5" trades the five best setups together. 5 · Re-calibration per timeframe

No timeframe holds PF 1.3 on unseen data, with any trailing stop

After the six presets failed, the calibration was redone for every timeframe from M1 to H1, on gold and on BTC, with part of the data held back. Each setup was scored on January 2025 – 15 May 2026 only: the lowest of six profit factors (three periods, each at normal and at stressed costs, at least 20 trades per period). The 10 best setups of each timeframe, one per direction, session and trend-filter combination and only with a score of 1.3 or more, were then run unchanged on 16 May – 13 November 2026. A timeframe passes with a profit factor of 1.3 or more on at least 100 of those unseen trades.

Run 1 used the EA's own trailing stop (multiples of the risk): 12,000 random setups per timeframe. Run 2 added ten more trailing families: 33,000 setups per timeframe.

6 · What this means

The EA is right; the pattern is not enough on its own

  • The EA is correct. On VT real ticks (Mar – mid-May), 100 of its 107 entries match a Python backtest entry within one bar, on the same side. Two independent price feeds also agree on the later months: 97% of the trades match and the six-preset totals are close.
  • The backtest numbers came from fitting, mainly to the gold rally of late 2025 and early 2026. After that period the results lose even before costs (VT PF 0.74 before commission and 0.67 at VT's $0.60 per lot, Jun – Nov 6).
  • No timeframe and no trailing stop changes that. Re-calibrated from M1 to H1 with eleven kinds of trailing stop, on gold and on BTC, nothing holds PF 1.3 on unseen data. The one BTC pattern that looked good in 2026 did not repeat on 2023 or 2024.
  • Recalibrating is not a reliable fix. Only one of five recalibration schedules passes, on four windows that lean on the rally. A schedule that works only for one window length is more likely luck than an edge.
