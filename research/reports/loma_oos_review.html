Loma Out-of-Sample Review

The six gold presets were tested on prices they were never fitted to: VT Markets real ticks in MetaTrader 5 (Codex's run, 1 Mar – 6 Nov 2026), a second data source (Dukascopy), and a rolling walk-forward recalibration on FTMO data. A full re-calibration of every timeframe on gold and BTC, with 23 trailing systems and part of the data held back, followed, and then a year-by-year test on long history (gold from 2013, BTC from 2018) with 59 entry conditions.

Verdict: do not trade these presets with real money.

The EA reproduces its backtest closely, so the code is not the problem. The edge the backtest showed did not continue after the data used to choose the presets: from June on, five of the six lose money on VT even before commission and the sixth (Top02) is about break-even there; on Dukascopy prices all six lose. Recalibrating every few months gives mixed results: one schedule out of five passes, but only on four windows that include the rally.

Year by year: no gold result, one BTC result. Set up on three years and traded on the next, BTC on M30 with the Stochastic turn rule scored 1.22 in 2023, 1.39 in 2024, 1.87 in 2025, and 1.26 on 267 trades in the 2026 months that were kept back (1.46 on 753 trades over all four unseen years). Its setups for the year ahead are in the BTC zip: demo first.

BTC: unproven, demo only. The four new BTC M5 presets passed the unseen months of 2026 (PF 1.42 on 133 trades) and lost on 2024 and 2023 (0.79 and 0.83). The earlier BTC01–BTC04 presets are withdrawn: they were fitted on a price file with a wrong clock.

6 · Year by year on long history

Year-by-year test

The method was then tested the way it would be traded: set up on three years, traded on the next year without changes, and rolled forward one year at a time. Gold: Dukascopy prices from 2013 (FTMO for 2025 to mid-May 2026), so ten unseen years, 2016 to 2025. BTC: Binance from 2018, so five unseen years, 2021 to 2025. The last months, 2026 to 13 November, were kept back and run once at the end, only for what had passed. Each year 12,000 setups per timeframe (M1 to H1) were scored on the three years before it with 59 entry conditions added one at a time: market regime, location, confirmation, timing, the XLZC rules, and three random halves of the signals as controls. Picks: the 10 best, one per direction, session and trend-filter family, each holding PF 1.3 in all three years at normal and stressed costs, and with an average cost under 1/8 of the risk. A cell passes with PF 1.3 or more over all its unseen years together, 1.1 or more stressed, at least two unseen years with 50 trades, and no such year below 1.0.

Costs: gold at VT Markets' own, a 1.5-pip spread (shaped by hour) and $0.60 commission per ounce; BTC a $10 spread. Both, and every dollar distance of a setup, are scaled with the price, so a setup means the same in 2016 as today. Swap is not charged. 944 cells were tested; with that many, a few pass by chance, which is why the final check exists. The random controls passed 0 of 48 times.

7 · What this means

The EA is right; the pattern is not enough on its own

  • The EA is correct. On VT real ticks (Mar – mid-May), 100 of its 107 entries match a Python backtest entry within one bar, on the same side. Two independent price feeds also agree on the later months: 97% of the trades match and the six-preset totals are close.
  • The backtest numbers came from fitting to January 2025 – mid-May 2026 as a whole: in the backtest the six presets earned in both halves of 2025 (PF 1.89 or more) and in 2026 (1.40 to 2.07). After that period the results lose even before costs (VT PF 0.74 before commission and 0.67 at VT's $0.60 per lot, Jun – Nov 6).
  • One result held year by year, on BTC. With 944 timeframe-and-condition cells tested on long history, no gold cell and 2 BTC cells passed; the better one, M30 with the Stochastic turn rule, also made money in the 2026 months it had not seen (1.26). It is a candidate for a demo, not a proven edge: the same rule did not pass on other timeframes.
  • No timeframe and no trailing stop changes that on gold. Re-calibrated from M1 to H1 with 23 trailing systems, the best setups of each gold timeframe score 0.67 to 1.08 on unseen data; four single timeframe-and-system combinations reached 1.31 to 1.57 in 2026 and all lost on July – December 2024. On BTC, M5 passed the 2026 months with the EA's own trailing (PF 1.42) and lost on 2023 and 2024, so it is a lead, not an edge.
  • Recalibrating is not a reliable fix. Only one of five recalibration schedules passes, on four windows that lean on the rally. A schedule that works only for one window length is more likely luck than an edge.
