Liquidity Zero-Cross Bearish: calibration, review and FlashGold comparison

The EA's rules replayed in Python on 480,000 M1 bars: 126,360 EA setups, 80,640 FlashGold filter settings and a 139,000-run paired comparison. Results are in R, multiples of the risk per trade; because every trade risks the same % of equity, profit factor (PF) in R equals PF in money. A multi-agent code review and an audit of the FlashGold port shaped every number on this page.

The test data is in broker server time, not UTC. The earlier presets told the EA the 12:00–16:30 window was GMT, which would have traded 2–3 hours later than calibrated. The calibrated presets now use server time. If your broker's server clock is not GMT+2/+3 with a New York close, shift the hours.

The two presets built from this research, both without the FlashGold filter. M2/H1 was chosen with 2025 data only, so its 2026 column is a true out-of-sample test. M3/H1 was chosen with all 17 months, so every column is in-sample.

What holds up out of sample

Setups chosen with 2025 data only, then measured on January–May 2026, which the choice never saw.

Picking the single best setup does not carry over: the top picks did worse in 2026 than the 51% base rate. Picking the trigger/bias pair does: the two best pair families on 2025 alone were M2/H1 and M3/H1, and both stayed profitable in 2026.

Method and limits

  • Data: FTMO-Server4 XAUUSD mid-price M1 bars, Jan 2 2025 – May 15 2026, broker server time (GMT+2/+3). Spreads are a constant mean per hour of day from the broker's measured hourly spreads.
  • Fills: shorts enter at the bid on the first tick after the signal bar; SL and TP trigger on the ask; when one bar touches both, the SL counts first. A trailing stop set and touched inside the same M1 bar counts when the bar closes beyond it; the pessimistic case is in the stress table.
  • FlashGold: a Python port of the indicator's MQL5 core, audited line by line. Not modelled: session-truncated bar ends and the live-observation commit of higher-timeframe values. The gate is read once, at the entry tick, as the EA now does.
  • Not modelled: news filter, commission, swap, slippage beyond the spread. Point distances are fixed while gold rose about 45% over the data.
  • This is a replica, not the MT5 Strategy Tester. Compile the EA and confirm in MT5 with real ticks before trading live.
